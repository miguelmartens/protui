# Short present-tense imperative phrase describing the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by `[title](file.md)`
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the forcing issue? Describe the situation, the constraints, and the
facts that were true when this was decided — not the decision itself.

State things that may change over time (costs, upstream behaviour, versions)
with the date attached, so a future reader can tell what has gone stale.

Include the options that were genuinely considered and why they were plausible.
A context section that lists only the chosen option is not showing its work.

## Decision

The decision, in active voice: "We will …".

One decision per record. If you find yourself writing "and also", that is a
second record.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder. Both. A record with only upsides
is a sales pitch, not a decision record.

Include:

- What this forces elsewhere in the codebase.
- What it rules out.
- Any follow-up decisions this creates.
- What would make us revisit it.
